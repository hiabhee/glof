## Rendering method

Both previews use the RGB bands B4/B3/B2 from `COPERNICUS/S2_HARMONIZED`, displayed using a same-day tile mosaic. The mosaic is required because the initial site envelope crosses a Sentinel-2 tile boundary; single granules leave missing pixels over part of the envelope.

ISRO/NRSC's authoritative immediate event assessment compares radar imagery from 28 September and 4 October 2023 and reports approximately 105 hectares drained. The Sentinel-2 pair above must not be substituted for that SAR-based event measurement.
