The selected RGI outline is the lake-terminating reference intersecting the South Lhonak lake area at the relevant western terminus. The underlying source has a 2000-era observation date, so it is deliberately presented as a baseline rather than a current outline.

## Lake boundary policy

No lake polygon has been approved for the application yet. ISRO/NRSC publishes official event maps and reports a major reduction in lake area during the October 2023 outburst, but a map image is not automatically a vector dataset licensed and documented for direct reuse.

The next lake-boundary source must provide, at minimum:

- a polygon geometry or an authorised downloadable GIS layer;
- a stated observation date;
- source, method, and licence/provenance;
- a quality check against the selected satellite scene.

Until then, the interface may show official maps as cited contextual evidence, but must not represent an extracted or hand-drawn lake shape as an inventory boundary.
