# South Lhonak Glacier boundary register

## Purpose

This register prevents the user interface from presenting a visual interpretation as a measured glacier-retreat result.

## Registered geometry

| Geometry | Observation date | Status | Permitted use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `RGI2000-v7.0-G-15-07986` | 26 Dec 2000 | Approved historical baseline | Reference overlay and glacier identity |

The RGI source is a single circa-2000 inventory outline. It must not be used alone to calculate a modern retreat rate.

## Intake requirements for later outlines

A later South Lhonak Glacier outline can enter GlacierLens only when it has all of the following:

1. A published or authorised inventory vector geometry.
2. Observation date and source imagery/survey record.
3. Delineation method and uncertainty statement.
4. A licence or citation permitting its use in the research demonstration.
5. A compatibility review against the RGI baseline.

## Candidate research context, not a vector source

Published South Lhonak studies provide important scientific context, but a paper image or a narrative retreat figure is not automatically registered as a reusable boundary. The next work item is to obtain a citable supplementary vector dataset or an official inventory geometry.
